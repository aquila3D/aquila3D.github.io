Predictive Analysis of Net Cancer Survival in OECD Countries — A Decision Tree Approach
R · French

Classification of 5-year net cancer survival rates (low / medium / high) across OECD countries using CONCORD program data (n = 2,098 observations). Models compared: CART decision tree, random forest, and multinomial logistic regression — all achieving 91.7% accuracy (κ = 0.87), with cancer type identified as the dominant predictive variable.

Data mining Decision tree (CART) Random forest OECD data KDD framework
Comparative Efficacy of Venous Leg Ulcer Treatments — A Bayesian Network Meta-Analysis
WinBUGS · R · English

Bayesian network meta-analysis (NMA) of 15 randomised controlled trials comparing 6 therapeutic strategies for venous leg ulcer healing. Lu & Ades random-effects model, MCMC (50,000 iterations, 3 chains), SUCRA ranking. Low-level laser therapy identified as optimal strategy (42.8% probability of ranking 1st). Concludes with a call for ICER modelling to inform HTA decisions.
